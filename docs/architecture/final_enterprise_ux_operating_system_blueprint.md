3. **Enterprise Diagnostic Query Engine**: Executes metadata AST queries across Knowledge Graph, Workflow Engine, Registry Engine, Spatial Engine, Carbon Engine, Risk Engine, Compliance Engine, and Digital Twin via **`Run Intelligence Query`**.

4. **9 Workspace-Centric Hubs**: Replaces page-centric views. Every workspace presents status, AI intelligence, timeline, spatial context, evidence, workflows, approvals, risks, documents, registry, carbon, financial impact, recommendations, and audit logs.

5. **Unified Spatial Command Center GIS Engine (`/dashboard/command-center`)**: 15 Metadata-Driven Layers across 4 Modes (`Micro Operations 30m Radius`, `Regional Operations`, `National Digital Twin`, `Global Climate Network`).

6. **Proactive AI Surface**: Surfaces Critical Risks, Anomalies, Predictions, Recommended Actions, Confidence, Priority, Financial Impact, Root Cause, and Counterfactual What-If before manual user searches.

7. **27 Autonomous Domain Agents**: 100% AI coverage across 30 climate operational domains.

8. **Token-Driven Enterprise Design System**: Standardized typography, spacing, elevation, motion, icons, badges, drawers, command palette (`Ctrl+K`), and cards.

9. **Navigation Simplification (3-Click Max)**: Inline editing, inline approvals, split panels, and contextual drawers.
